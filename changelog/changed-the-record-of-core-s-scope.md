- One new record of core's scope states the two-consumer rule and supersedes ADR-0023: a
  consumer is a source or the host by any code it runs, the rule judges every piece that a caller
  can name, and one consumer is enough for the family's contract alone, so that the maintainer's
  decision on a step's design question is no reason of its own any more. A count of the
  consumers is a report by which nothing leaves, and the definition of done, the review
  checklist's item 11 and conventions §15 cite the record in place of ADR-0023 and ADR-0058.
  (ADR-0059, ADR-0058, ADR-0052, §15, https://github.com/xippios/sdk-core/issues/473, https://github.com/xippios/sdk-core/issues/485)
